# d=3: multiscale and pair-covariance fingerprints

This note tests two richer fingerprints for the d=3 adaptive decoder against the adaptive-stale ensemble from note 07 (`07_ensembles.md`, formerly `results/report6.md`). Only the fingerprint channels change. Everything else is the d=3 adaptive-stale setup: `--stale-prob 0.3`, `data/d3_wide`, h48/l4, fp32, batch 512, 60000 steps, model seeds 0, 1 and 2.

Every number here comes from `results/exp7/report7_tables.md` (generated by `results/exp7/report7.py`), `results/exp7/STATUS.txt` and `results/exp7/check7.txt`. Nothing was trained or evaluated for this note. In the tables, "baseline" means the adaptive-stale models of note 07; their results are reused from `results/exp6`, not re-run.

Statistics. Ensemble = mean of the 3 seeds' logits, thresholded at 0 (fixed in advance, as in note 07). "Live" means each model's own causal fingerprint buffer. "Frozen" means every channel comes from the nominal test set's fingerprint shots. The fingerprint updates every 200 shots. Streams are pooled over stream seeds 0-4. Paired tests are exact two-sided McNemar on shared shots between ensembles. diff = LER(a) − LER(b), so negative means a is better. As in earlier notes, p in 0.001-0.05 counts as weak evidence and there is no multiple-testing correction. The best WindowDEM is picked per phase after the fact.

## Summary

1. **Decision rule: both variants fail.** B (multiscale) meets the win condition but fails the no-loss condition on one cell only: hot_qubit later steps, +0.0002 (p = 0.0005). C (multiscale+pairs) meets the win condition but fails the no-loss condition on bursts_big drift (+0.0050, p = 8e-21) and bursts_big later (+0.0082, p = 2e-43).
2. **B helps a lot at burst onset.** On bursts and bursts_big, B beats the baseline in every phase: onset −0.0171 and −0.0175 (p ≤ 4e-67), with smaller gains later and after. On hot_qubit it wins at onset (−0.0033, p = 2e-05) and loses by +0.0002 on later and drift steps. On telegraph it ties.
3. **C is the best or tied for best everywhere except bursts_big.** C beats the baseline and B on bursts and hot_qubit drift steps (p ≤ 1e-11; vs B on telegraph only weakly, p = 0.004), and on the 2-hot-qubit test set (−0.00112 vs baseline, p = 1e-11). On bursts_big it beats the baseline only at onset and after, and loses to B in every burst phase (drift +0.0112, p = 1e-113).
4. **Both variants remove the d=3 after-burst cost.** The baseline ensemble is worse than static MWPM after bursts end (bursts +0.0010, bursts_big +0.0026). B and C tie static MWPM there (p ≥ 0.2).
5. **Live vs frozen.** The live fingerprint helps B and C more than it helps the baseline on every burst regime. Part of the larger gap comes from B and C doing worse when frozen.

## Decision rule (fixed in advance)

The rule was set before the run. A variant passes if both conditions hold:

- **Win:** its live ensemble beats the baseline live ensemble at onset or after on bursts or bursts_big, with p < 0.001.
- **No loss:** it is not worse than the baseline on later or drift steps in any regime (bursts, bursts_big, hot_qubit, telegraph) at p < 0.001.

The tests use live ensembles and pooled streams.

| variant | verdict | wins (p < 0.001) | losses (p < 0.001) |
|---|---|---|---|
| B multiscale | **FAILS** | bursts onset −0.0171 (3e-112); bursts after −0.0014 (8e-09); bursts_big onset −0.0175 (4e-67); bursts_big after −0.0030 (3e-26) | hot_qubit later +0.0002 (p = 0.0005) |
| C multiscale+pairs | **FAILS** | bursts onset −0.0180 (2e-110); bursts after −0.0013 (5e-07); bursts_big onset −0.0046 (0.0001); bursts_big after −0.0024 (2e-14) | bursts_big drift +0.0050 (8e-21); bursts_big later +0.0082 (2e-43) |

Outcome, stated exactly: both variants fail. B fails only on hot_qubit later steps, by +0.0002 (p = 0.0005). On hot_qubit drift steps B is also worse by +0.0002, but p = 0.004 is above the 0.001 threshold, so that cell does not count. C fails on two bursts_big cells, and by much more. Neither variant replaces the baseline under the rule as written.

- The live fingerprint matters more for B and C than for the baseline, mainly at onset (bursts −0.0248 → −0.0440 / −0.0464).

Frozen ensembles on bursts_big drift score 0.2530 (baseline), 0.2539 (B) and 0.2714 (C); on hot_qubit drift 0.0307, 0.0326 and 0.0343. These are unpaired comparisons: the tables have no frozen-vs-frozen tests between variants. The richer fingerprints lean on the fingerprint more, so they lose more when it is wrong.

- On telegraph, live and frozen still nearly tie (≤ 0.0003) for all three.

## Hypothesis (untested)

C's 8 covariance channels may take values outside their training range on bursts_big, because covariance grows with noise strength. bursts_big is the strongest-noise regime: static MWPM drift LER is 0.378 there, against 0.174 on bursts. If the channels are out of range, C could do well on milder regimes and then fail only on bursts_big, which is what the tables show. This has not been tested. Nothing in the files used here shows the channel values or the training range. One point does not fit: C is also worse than B with the frozen nominal fingerprint on bursts_big (0.2714 vs 0.2539, unpaired). The channels should be in range in that case, so out-of-range values would not explain the whole gap.

## Limitations

- One ensemble (one trio of seeds) per variant, and 60k training steps (the d=3 budget). The d=5 notes showed that conclusions can change with training length.
- The decision rule counts any loss with p < 0.001, however small. B's single failing cell is +0.0002. The rule was applied as written.
- McNemar measures shot noise. Variation between seeds shows only in the std rows.
- The best WindowDEM is picked per phase after the fact.
- Many comparisons, no multiple-testing correction.
- The long window length and the choice of the 8 covariance pairs are not stated in the source files for this note.
